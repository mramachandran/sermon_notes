# Cain

*The first son and the first murderer, warned by God that sin was crouching at his door.*

Cain was the firstborn of Adam and Eve and the first person born in the Bible. He worked the ground like his father. When God accepted his brother Abel's offering but not his own, Cain grew angry. God warned him that sin was waiting to master him, but Cain killed Abel in the field. Even then, God questioned him, judged him, and protected him from being killed in turn. Cain went out from God's presence, built a city, and fathered a line known for both skill and violence. His story shows how quickly sin grew after Eden, and how God speaks to us before we fall.

## The life of Cain

*Genesis gives no dates or ages for Cain. The order below follows the text.*

**After Eden — Born to Eve.** Eve bears her first son and says, "I have gotten a man from the LORD." *Genesis 4:1*

**Grown — Works the ground.** Abel keeps sheep, "but Cain was a tiller of the ground." *Genesis 4:2*

**Later — Brings an offering.** Cain brings fruit of the ground to the LORD. God respects Abel's offering but not Cain's, and Cain's face falls in anger. *Genesis 4:3–5*

**Then — Warned by God.** God asks why he is angry and warns that sin is lying at the door, but that he must rule over it. *Genesis 4:6–7*

**In the field — Kills Abel.** Cain talks with his brother, and in the field he rises up against Abel and kills him. *Genesis 4:8*

**Afterward — Judged and marked.** God asks, "Where is Abel thy brother?" Cain says, "Am I my brother's keeper?" God curses him to be a wanderer. When Cain fears being killed, God sets a mark on him for protection. *Genesis 4:9–15*

**East of Eden — Settles in Nod.** Cain goes out from the presence of the LORD to the land of Nod. He fathers Enoch and builds a city named after him. *Genesis 4:16–17*

### The world around them

Genesis 4 describes the first family living east of Eden. Farming and herding were the two basic ways of life in the ancient Near East, and Genesis shows both from the start. Offerings of produce and of animals were made across the ancient world long before the law of Moses gave rules for them. The land of Nod appears on no known map. Its name sounds like the Hebrew word for "wandering" (Genesis 4:12, 16), which may be the point. Early cities were small walled settlements, often built around one family or clan.

## Key activities

*Genesis does not say why God rejected Cain's offering. Some point to the kind of gift; Hebrews 11:4 points to faith. Genesis also does not name Cain's wife. A common view is that she was one of Adam's daughters (Genesis 5:4). Scripture does not say what the mark was.*

1. **Worked the ground.** He took up the work given to Adam after the fall. *Genesis 3:23; 4:2*
2. **Brought an offering.** He brought "of the fruit of the ground" to the LORD, but it was not accepted. *Genesis 4:3–5*
3. **Grew angry.** He was very angry, and his face fell. *Genesis 4:5*
4. **Ignored God's warning.** God urged him to do well and to master sin before it mastered him. *Genesis 4:6–7*
5. **Killed his brother.** He attacked Abel in the field, the first murder in Scripture. *Genesis 4:8*
6. **Denied and dodged.** He lied ("I know not") and asked, "Am I my brother's keeper?" *Genesis 4:9*
7. **Cried out under judgment.** He said, "My punishment is greater than I can bear," and feared for his life. *Genesis 4:13–14*
8. **Built a city.** He fathered Enoch and named a city after him. *Genesis 4:17*

### Words and places

- **Offering.** A gift brought to God. Genesis 4 uses a general word that the law later uses for grain offerings (Genesis 4:3–5; Leviticus 2:1).
- **Mark.** A sign God set on Cain so that no one would kill him. Scripture does not say what it was (Genesis 4:15).
- **Nod.** The land east of Eden where Cain settled. The name sounds like "wandering" (Genesis 4:16).
- **Enoch (the city).** The first city named in the Bible, called after Cain's son. This is not the Enoch of Genesis 5 (Genesis 4:17).

- **Day 2.** Genesis 4:8–16. How did God show both justice and mercy?

## Across Scripture

*Direct references come first. Thematic echoes are study connections, not places where the text names Cain.*

### Old Testament

- **Genesis 4:1–16.** The whole account of Cain
- **Genesis 4:17–24.** His line, down to boastful, violent Lamech
- **Genesis 4:25.** Seth given "instead of Abel, whom Cain slew"
- **Echo: Genesis 9:6.** Shedding blood forbidden; humans bear God's image

### New Testament

- **Hebrews 11:4.** Abel's "more excellent sacrifice than Cain"
- **1 John 3:12.** "Not as Cain, who was of that wicked one"
- **Jude 11.** False teachers "have gone in the way of Cain"
- **Echo: Matthew 5:21–22.** Anger in the heart linked to murder

## For discussion

1. **Observe:** What did God say to Cain before he killed Abel (Genesis 4:6–7)?
2. **Understand:** Why do you think God protected Cain with a mark?
3. **Apply:** Sin waits at the door. What "doors" in your life need watching?
4. **Apply:** "Am I my brother's keeper?" Who has God given you to care for this week?
