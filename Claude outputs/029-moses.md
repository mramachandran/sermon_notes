# Moses

*The reluctant shepherd who led Israel out of Egypt and spoke with God face to face.*

Moses was born a Hebrew slave under a death sentence, raised in Pharaoh's household, and trained as a shepherd in the desert. At eighty he met God in a burning bush and was sent to say, "Let my people go." Through him God brought Israel out of slavery, gave the Law at Sinai, and built a nation in the wilderness. He was prophet, lawgiver and intercessor, yet also a man who argued, lost his temper, and was kept out of the Promised Land. The New Testament names him more than any other Old Testament person.

## The life of Moses

*Dates are approximate (BC), following the early Exodus date, c. 1446 BC (1 Kings 6:1). Many scholars prefer c. 1260 BC, about 185 years later.*

**c. 1526 BC · Birth — Hidden and drawn out.** Hidden from Pharaoh's decree in a basket among the reeds, he is found and raised by Pharaoh's daughter. *Exodus 1:22–2:10*

**c. 1486 BC · Age 40 — Flees to Midian.** He kills an Egyptian who was beating a Hebrew, and flees when Pharaoh seeks his life. *Exodus 2:11–15; Acts 7:23–29*

**c. 1486–1446 BC · Age 40 to 80 — Shepherd in Midian.** He marries Zipporah and keeps Jethro's flock in the desert for forty years. *Exodus 2:16–22; 3:1; Acts 7:29–30*

**c. 1446 BC · Age 80 — The burning bush.** At Horeb God reveals his name, "I AM THAT I AM," and sends a reluctant Moses to Egypt. *Exodus 3:1–4:17; 7:7*

**c. 1446 BC · Age 80 — Out of Egypt.** Ten plagues fall and Israel leaves after the Passover, crossing the sea on dry ground. *Exodus 5:1–15:21*

**c. 1446–1445 BC · At Sinai — Law and tabernacle.** He receives the Law, breaks the tablets over the golden calf, and builds the tabernacle. *Exodus 19–40*

**c. 1445 BC · Second year — Turned back at Kadesh.** Ten spies spread fear, and that generation is sentenced to wander forty years. *Numbers 10:11; 13:1–14:38*

**c. 1406 BC · Age 120 — Death on Nebo.** He commissions Joshua, sees the land from Mount Nebo, and dies; God buries him. *Deuteronomy 31–34*

## Key activities

*Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers and Deuteronomy tell Moses' story together. Deuteronomy 1–4 retells some events from Moses' own point of view.*

1. **Chose God's people over Egypt.** He refused to be called the son of Pharaoh's daughter. *Exodus 2:11–12; Hebrews 11:24–26*
2. **Protected the helpless.** At a desert well he defended seven sisters and watered their flock. *Exodus 2:16–17*
3. **Argued with God, then obeyed.** "I am slow of speech, and of a slow tongue." God answered each objection. *Exodus 3:11–4:17*
4. **Confronted Pharaoh.** He carried one message from God: let my people go. *Exodus 5:1–11:10*
5. **Led the first Passover.** Each household marked its doorposts with a lamb's blood. *Exodus 12:1–28*
6. **Stood firm at the sea.** "Fear ye not, stand still, and see the salvation of the LORD." *Exodus 14:13–15:18*
7. **Shared the load.** He appointed judges on Jethro's advice; later seventy elders helped him. *Exodus 18:13–26; Numbers 11:16–17, 24–29*
8. **Received the Law.** On Sinai he received the Ten Commandments and sealed the covenant with blood. *Exodus 19:1–24:8; 20:1–17*
9. **Stood in the gap.** After the golden calf he asked to be blotted out of God's book in Israel's place. *Exodus 32:11–14, 30–32; Psalm 106:23*
10. **Spoke with God as a friend.** "The LORD spake unto Moses face to face, as a man speaketh unto his friend." *Exodus 33:11, 18–23; 34:5–8*
11. **Struck the rock.** At Meribah he struck the rock in anger and was barred from the land. *Numbers 20:7–12*
12. **Handed on the work.** He laid his hands on Joshua and urged Israel to "choose life." *Numbers 27:18–23; Deuteronomy 30:19; 31:7–8*

## Across Scripture

*Direct references come first. Thematic echoes are study connections, not places where the text names Moses.*

### Old Testament

- **Exodus – Deuteronomy.** His life from birth to death
- **Joshua 1:1–9.** "As I was with Moses, so I will be with thee"
- **Psalm 90.** "A Prayer of Moses the man of God"
- **Psalm 106:23.** Moses "stood before him in the breach"
- **Jeremiah 15:1.** Moses and Samuel as great intercessors
- **Malachi 4:4.** "Remember ye the law of Moses my servant"

### New Testament

- **Matthew 17:1–8.** With Elijah at the transfiguration
- **John 1:17.** Law by Moses; grace and truth by Jesus Christ
- **John 3:14.** The serpent lifted up in the wilderness
- **Acts 7:20–44.** Stephen retells his life
- **Hebrews 11:23–29.** His faith praised
- **Tradition.** Moses wrote the first five books; many think they were completed later

## For discussion

1. **Observe:** What reasons did Moses give for not going back to Egypt (Exodus 3:11–4:13)?
2. **Understand:** Why do you think God kept Moses out of the land (Numbers 20:7–12)?
3. **Apply:** Who could help carry something you are holding alone, as Jethro helped Moses?
4. **Apply:** Moses pleaded for people who let him down. Who could you pray for by name?
