# Aaron

*Moses' older brother and spokesman, and the first high priest of Israel.*

Aaron was three years older than Moses, and God gave him to his brother as a voice when Moses said he could not speak well. Together they stood before Pharaoh, and Aaron's rod swallowed the magicians' rods. At Sinai he became Israel's first high priest, the man who would bring the people's offerings to God and God's blessing to the people. Yet the same Aaron made the golden calf, joined Miriam in criticizing Moses, and shared Moses' failure at Meribah. His life shows both the dignity and the weakness of priesthood. The New Testament uses his office to explain the work of Christ, a greater high priest.

## The life of Aaron

*Dates are approximate (BC), on the early Exodus date, c. 1446 BC; the late date moves them about 185 years later. Ages from Exodus 7:7 and Numbers 33:39.*

**c. 1529 BC · Birth — Born in Egypt.** Aaron is born to Amram and Jochebed, three years before Moses. *Exodus 6:20; 7:7*

**c. 1446 BC · Age 83 — Meets his brother.** Aaron meets Moses at the mountain of God, and they tell Israel's elders what God has said. *Exodus 4:14–16, 27–31*

**c. 1446 BC · Age 83 — Before Pharaoh.** His rod swallows the magicians' rods, and he stretches it out to bring the first plagues. *Exodus 7:1–20; 8:5–6, 16–17*

**c. 1446 BC · Wilderness — Holds up Moses' hands.** At Rephidim Aaron and Hur hold up Moses' hands until sunset, and Israel wins. *Exodus 17:8–13*

**c. 1446 BC · At Sinai — The golden calf.** He sees the God of Israel with the elders, but later gives in and makes a golden calf. *Exodus 24:9–11; 32:1–6*

**c. 1445 BC · Second year — Ordained high priest.** He offers the first sacrifices, and fire comes from the LORD; that day Nadab and Abihu die. *Exodus 40:17; Leviticus 8:1–10:7*

**c. 1445 BC · Hazeroth — Speaks against Moses.** With Miriam he criticizes Moses, then pleads for her when she is struck with leprosy. *Numbers 11:35; 12:1–12*

**c. 1406 BC · Age 123 — Dies on Mount Hor.** His garments are put on Eleazar, and Israel mourns him thirty days. *Numbers 20:23–29; 33:38–39*

## Key activities

*Some events, such as Korah's rebellion and the budding rod, are undated in Numbers.*

1. **Spoke for Moses.** "I know that he can speak well." *Exodus 4:14–16; 7:1–2*
2. **Worked signs before Pharaoh.** He threw down his rod and stretched it over Egypt's waters. *Exodus 7:10–12, 19–20*
3. **Held up his brother's hands.** With Hur he held up Moses' hands all day at Rephidim. *Exodus 17:10–12*
4. **Saw God on the mountain.** He went up Sinai with the elders, and they saw the God of Israel and ate and drank. *Exodus 24:1, 9–11*
5. **Made the golden calf.** "I cast it into the fire, and there came out this calf." *Exodus 32:1–6, 21–24*
6. **Was clothed for glory.** He wore the ephod, the breastplate, and "HOLINESS TO THE LORD." *Exodus 28:1–38; Leviticus 8:6–12*
7. **Blessed the people.** After the first sacrifices he lifted his hands and blessed Israel, and God's fire consumed the offering. *Leviticus 9:22–24; Numbers 6:22–27*
8. **Held his peace.** When fire killed Nadab and Abihu, "Aaron held his peace." *Leviticus 10:1–3*
9. **Challenged Moses.** He and Miriam asked, "Hath the LORD indeed spoken only by Moses?" Then he begged Moses to pray for her. *Numbers 12:1–12*
10. **Stopped a plague.** He ran with his censer into the camp, "and he stood between the dead and the living; and the plague was stayed." *Numbers 16:46–48*
11. **Was confirmed by a budding rod.** Among twelve rods, his alone budded, blossomed and bore almonds overnight. *Numbers 17:1–10*
12. **Handed on the priesthood.** On Mount Hor his garments were placed on Eleazar before he died. *Numbers 20:25–28*

## Across Scripture

*Direct references come first. Thematic echoes are study connections, not places where the text names Aaron.*

### Old Testament

- **Exodus 28–29; Leviticus 8–10.** His garments and ordination
- **Deuteronomy 9:20.** Moses prayed for him after the calf
- **Deuteronomy 10:6.** His death at Mosera, probably near Hor
- **Psalm 77:20.** God led his people "by the hand of Moses and Aaron"
- **Psalm 133:2.** His anointing a picture of unity
- **Micah 6:4.** God sent "Moses, Aaron, and Miriam"

### New Testament

- **Luke 1:5.** Elisabeth is "of the daughters of Aaron"
- **Hebrews 5:4.** Called of God, "as was Aaron"
- **Hebrews 7:11–28.** Christ a priest after Melchisedec, not Aaron
- **Hebrews 9:4.** His budded rod kept in the ark
- **Echo: Hebrews 4:14–16.** Christ the "great high priest"
