# Bezalel

*The craftsman God filled with his Spirit to build the tabernacle.*

Bezalel was a craftsman from the tribe of Judah. At Mount Sinai, God called him by name to build the tabernacle, the tent where God would dwell among his people. He is the first person Scripture names as "filled … with the spirit of God," and the Spirit gave him wisdom and skill in gold, silver, bronze, stone and wood. With his partner Aholiab and many skilled workers, he turned the people's generous gifts into the ark, the altars, the lampstand and the tent itself. Bezalel never speaks in Scripture, and he never leads a battle. His story shows that practical, creative work can be a calling from God.

## The life of Bezalel

*Dates are approximate (BC) and follow the early Exodus date, c. 1446 BC. On the late date, move them about 185 years later. The tabernacle was set up on the first day of the first month of the second year (Exodus 40:17).*

**c. 1446 BC · At Sinai — Called by name.** While Moses is on the mountain, God names Bezalel as the builder of the tabernacle and all its furnishings, with Aholiab to help him. *Exodus 31:1–11*

**c. 1446 BC · After the golden calf — Presented to the people.** Moses tells Israel that the LORD has called Bezalel and filled him with his Spirit. God has also given him and Aholiab the ability to teach others. *Exodus 35:30–35*

**c. 1446 BC · At Sinai — Receives the gifts.** The people bring freewill offerings every morning. Soon the workers report that there is more than enough, and Moses tells the people to stop giving. *Exodus 36:1–7*

**c. 1446–1445 BC · At Sinai — Builds.** Bezalel and the skilled workers make the curtains, boards and veil. He makes the ark and mercy seat, the table, the lampstand, the altars and the laver. *Exodus 36:8–38:20*

**c. 1445 BC · Second year — The work is finished.** The finished pieces are brought to Moses. He sees that everything is done as the LORD commanded and blesses the workers. The tabernacle is raised. *Exodus 38:22; 39:32–43; 40:17*

### The world around them

The tabernacle was a portable tent-temple about 45 feet long, set inside a courtyard of curtains. Israel carried it through the wilderness and set it up at the center of the camp. Its materials were costly: gold, silver, bronze, fine linen, dyed wool and acacia wood. Exodus says the Israelites left Egypt with silver, gold and clothing given by the Egyptians (Exodus 12:35–36). Egypt had skilled goldsmiths and weavers, and many think Hebrew slaves had learned such crafts there. The ark itself was small, a chest about 45 inches long.

## Key activities

*Some think Bezalel's grandfather Hur is the same Hur who held up Moses' hands (Exodus 17:10–12; 24:14). The text does not say, and Hur was a common name.*

1. **Was called by name.** God chose him personally for this work. *Exodus 31:2; 35:30*
2. **Was filled with the Spirit.** God gave him wisdom, understanding and knowledge for craftsmanship. *Exodus 31:3–5; 35:31–33*
3. **Taught others.** God put it in his heart to teach, along with Aholiab. *Exodus 35:34–35*
4. **Led a team.** He worked with Aholiab and "every wise hearted man" whom God had gifted. *Exodus 36:1–2, 8*
5. **Made the ark.** He made the ark of acacia wood overlaid with gold, the mercy seat, and the two cherubim. *Exodus 37:1–9*
6. **Made the furnishings.** He made the table, the golden lampstand, the incense altar, the anointing oil and the incense. *Exodus 37:10–29*
7. **Made the altar and laver.** He built the bronze altar, and he made the laver from the mirrors of the women who served at the door. *Exodus 38:1–8*
8. **Did everything as commanded.** He "made all that the LORD commanded Moses." *Exodus 38:22*

### Words and places

- **Tabernacle.** The portable "dwelling" or tent where God met Israel (Exodus 25:8–9; 40:34).
- **Ark of the testimony.** A gold-covered chest holding the tablets of the Law (Exodus 25:10–22).
- **Mercy seat.** The gold lid of the ark, where the blood was sprinkled on the Day of Atonement (Exodus 25:17; Leviticus 16:14).
- **Shittim wood.** Acacia, a hard desert wood (Exodus 25:10).

## Across Scripture

*Direct references come first. Thematic echoes are study connections, not places where the text names Bezalel.*

### Old Testament

- **Exodus 31:1–11.** God calls and equips him
- **Exodus 35:30–38:23.** He and the workers build the tabernacle
- **1 Chronicles 2:18–20.** His family line in Judah
- **2 Chronicles 1:5.** His bronze altar still at Gibeon in Solomon's day
- **Ezra 10:30.** A different Bezaleel, after the exile
- **Echo: 1 Kings 7:13–14.** Hiram, "filled with wisdom," works bronze for the temple

### New Testament

- Bezalel is not named in the New Testament.
- **Hebrews 8:5** and **Acts 7:44.** Moses made the tabernacle according to the pattern shown on the mountain.
- **Hebrews 9:1–5.** The tabernacle and its furniture are described.
- **Echoes.** The Spirit gives different gifts for the good of all (1 Corinthians 12:4–11). "Whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord" (Colossians 3:23).

## For discussion

1. **Observe:** What words does God use to describe the gifts he gave Bezalel (Exodus 31:3–5)?
2. **Understand:** Why do you think God filled a craftsman with his Spirit, not only prophets and leaders?
3. **Apply:** What practical skill has God given you? How could you use it to serve your church or neighbors this month?
4. **Apply:** Bezalel also taught others. Who could you train or encourage in a skill you have?
