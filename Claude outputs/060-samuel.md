# Samuel

*The last of the judges and a faithful prophet, who anointed Israel's first two kings.*

Samuel was born in answer to his mother Hannah's prayer and grew up serving God at the tabernacle in Shiloh. As a boy he heard the LORD call him by name, and all Israel came to know him as a prophet. He called Israel away from idols and judged the nation all his life, traveling a yearly circuit of towns. When the people demanded a king, Samuel warned them and prayed, and then he anointed Saul. When Saul disobeyed, Samuel rebuked him, and later he anointed young David. Samuel bridged the time of the judges and the time of the kings. Scripture remembers him beside Moses as a man who prayed for his people.

## The life of Samuel

*Dates are rough. The house chronology places Samuel's birth c. 1100 BC and Saul's reign c. 1050–1010 BC. First Samuel gives few ages or years for his life.*

**c. 1100 BC · Infancy — Asked of the LORD.** Hannah prays for a son and vows to give him to God. When Samuel is weaned, she brings him to Eli at Shiloh. There the child ministers before the LORD, wearing a linen ephod. *1 Samuel 1:20–28; 2:11, 18*

**Childhood — Called by name.** In days when "the word of the LORD was precious," God calls Samuel by night. Taught by Eli, he answers, "Speak; for thy servant heareth," and receives a hard message about Eli's house. *1 Samuel 3:1–18*

**Growing up — Known as a prophet.** The LORD is with him and lets none of his words fall to the ground. All Israel from Dan to Beersheba knows that Samuel is established as a prophet of the LORD. *1 Samuel 3:19–4:1*

**Twenty years on — Victory at Mizpeh.** After the ark has stayed twenty years at Kirjath-jearim, Samuel calls Israel to put away foreign gods. He prays at Mizpeh, the LORD thunders against the Philistines, and Samuel sets up a stone called Ebenezer. *1 Samuel 7:2–14*

**All his life — Judges Israel.** Each year he travels a circuit to Bethel, Gilgal and Mizpeh, judging Israel in each place. Then he returns home to Ramah, where he builds an altar to the LORD. *1 Samuel 7:15–17*

**c. 1050 BC · Old age — Anoints Saul.** His sons take bribes, and the elders ask for a king. God tells Samuel they have rejected God, not him. Samuel warns them, then anoints Saul and presents him at Mizpeh. *1 Samuel 8:1–10:25*

**Saul's reign — Rebukes and grieves.** Samuel rebukes Saul for offering sacrifice without him, and later for sparing Agag and the best of the spoil: "to obey is better than sacrifice." Then he mourns for Saul. *1 Samuel 13:8–14; 15:1–35*

**Late in Saul's reign — Anoints David and dies.** Sent by God to Bethlehem, he anoints Jesse's youngest son, David. Later he shelters David at Ramah. When Samuel dies, all Israel mourns and buries him at his house in Ramah. *1 Samuel 16:1–13; 19:18–24; 25:1*

## Key activities

*After Samuel's death, Saul asks a medium at Endor to bring him up, and "Samuel" speaks to Saul (1 Samuel 28:3–20). Many Christians believe God truly allowed Samuel to appear; others think it was a deception or a spirit posing as him.*

1. **Ministered from childhood.** As a boy he served before the LORD at Shiloh, girded with a linen ephod. *1 Samuel 2:18*
2. **Listened to God.** He answered God's call and told Eli the hard message in full. *1 Samuel 3:10–18*
3. **Called Israel to repent.** He urged them to put away Baalim and Ashtaroth and serve the LORD only. *1 Samuel 7:3–4*
4. **Prayed for the nation.** At Mizpeh he cried to the LORD for Israel, and the LORD heard him. *1 Samuel 7:5–10*
5. **Set up Ebenezer.** He named the stone, saying, "Hitherto hath the LORD helped us." *1 Samuel 7:12*
6. **Judged Israel all his life.** He rode a yearly circuit and built an altar at Ramah. *1 Samuel 7:15–17*
7. **Warned against a king.** He told the people how a king would take their sons, daughters, fields and flocks. *1 Samuel 8:10–18*
8. **Anointed Saul.** He poured oil on Saul's head and kissed him. *1 Samuel 10:1*
9. **Defended his integrity.** At Gilgal he asked whose ox or ass he had taken, and the people said he had defrauded no one. *1 Samuel 12:1–5*
10. **Kept praying.** "God forbid that I should sin against the LORD in ceasing to pray for you." *1 Samuel 12:23*
11. **Rebuked Saul.** He declared that God had rejected Saul as king because Saul had rejected God's word. *1 Samuel 15:22–28*
12. **Anointed David.** He learned that "the LORD looketh on the heart" and anointed Jesse's youngest son. *1 Samuel 16:6–13*

## Across Scripture

*Direct references come first. Thematic echoes are study connections, not places where the text names Samuel.*

### Old Testament

- **1 Samuel 1–25.** This is the main account. His death is reported in 1 Samuel 25:1 and again in 28:3.
- **1 Chronicles 6:27–28, 33.** Samuel and his sons are listed among the Levites descended from Kohath.
- **1 Chronicles 9:22** and **1 Chronicles 29:29.** He is called "Samuel the seer," and David's acts are written in "the book of Samuel the seer."
- **Psalm 99:6** and **Jeremiah 15:1.** Samuel is named with Moses as one who called on God for his people.
- **2 Chronicles 35:18.** No Passover had been kept like Josiah's since "the days of Samuel the prophet."

### New Testament

- **Acts 3:24.** Peter says "all the prophets from Samuel" foretold these days.
- **Acts 13:20.** Paul says God gave Israel judges "until Samuel the prophet."
- **Hebrews 11:32.** Samuel is named among the heroes of faith.
- **Echoes.** The boy Samuel "was in favour both with the LORD, and also with men" (1 Samuel 2:26). Luke says the boy Jesus grew "in favour with God and man" (Luke 2:52).
- **Tradition.** The Talmud credits Samuel with writing Judges, Ruth and part of the book that bears his name. Scripture does not say who wrote them.

## For discussion

1. **Observe:** What did Samuel set up after the victory at Mizpeh, and what did he say (1 Samuel 7:12)?
2. **Understand:** Samuel warned against a king, yet he anointed Saul and kept praying for the people. What does that show about his heart?
3. **Apply:** Samuel said failing to pray for others would be sin (1 Samuel 12:23). Whom will you commit to pray for this week?
4. **Apply:** "The LORD looketh on the heart" (1 Samuel 16:7). Where do you judge by outward looks, and how could you look deeper?
