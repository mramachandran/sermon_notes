# Jonathan

*The king's son who could have been jealous, and chose to be a friend.*

Jonathan was the son of Saul, named first among his sons and in line for Israel's throne. He was a brave soldier who trusted God enough to attack a Philistine garrison with only his armor-bearer beside him. Then a shepherd boy named David killed Goliath, and the people began to love David more than anyone. Jonathan had every reason to see David as a rival. Instead he made a covenant of friendship with him, gave him his own robe and weapons, and defended him before his angry father. He stayed loyal both to his father and to his friend, and he died beside Saul in battle. Few people in Scripture show so clearly what it looks like to be glad when God raises up someone else.

## The life of Jonathan

*Dates are approximate (BC). Scripture does not give Jonathan's age, and the events of Saul's reign are hard to date exactly, so most entries use labels.*

**Early in Saul's reign — Strikes the garrison at Geba.** Jonathan commands a thousand men at Gibeah. He attacks a Philistine garrison at Geba, and the Philistines gather a vast army against Israel in reply. *1 Samuel 13:2–5*

**Early in Saul's reign — Climbs to Michmash.** With only his armor-bearer, Jonathan climbs to a Philistine outpost. They kill about twenty men, the ground shakes, and panic spreads through the Philistine camp. *1 Samuel 14:1–23*

**The same day — Tastes the honey.** Not knowing his father's oath, Jonathan eats some wild honey. Saul wants him put to death, but the army refuses and rescues him. *1 Samuel 14:24–45*

**After Goliath's defeat — Makes a covenant.** Jonathan loves David as his own soul. He makes a covenant with him and gives him his robe, garments, sword, bow and belt. *1 Samuel 18:1–4*

**Saul's rage grows — Defends David.** Jonathan speaks well of David to Saul and wins a brief peace. Later Saul throws a spear at Jonathan himself, and Jonathan warns David with an arrow signal. *1 Samuel 19:1–7; 20:1–42*

**David in hiding — The last meeting.** Jonathan finds David in a wood in the wilderness of Ziph. He strengthens him in God and says David will be king, with Jonathan next to him. *1 Samuel 23:15–18*

**c. 1010 BC — Dies on Mount Gilboa.** The Philistines kill Jonathan and two of his brothers in battle. Saul dies the same day. Their bodies are hung on the wall of Beth-shan until the men of Jabesh-gilead recover them. *1 Samuel 31:1–13*

**Afterwards — Mourned and honored.** David laments Saul and Jonathan in a song. Years later he shows kindness to Jonathan's son and buries Jonathan's bones in the land of Benjamin. *2 Samuel 1:17–27; 9:1–13; 21:12–14*

## Key activities

*Jonathan's love for David is a covenant friendship between two men pledged to each other before God. David's lament calls it "wonderful, passing the love of women" (2 Samuel 1:26), a way of speaking about the depth of their loyalty.*

1. **Led the first blow.** He struck the Philistine garrison at Geba, opening the war for Israel's freedom. *1 Samuel 13:3*
2. **Trusted God against the odds.** He told his armor-bearer, "there is no restraint to the LORD to save by many or by few." *1 Samuel 14:6*
3. **Asked God for a sign.** He agreed that if the Philistines said "Come up," they would go, and took it as the LORD's answer. *1 Samuel 14:8–12*
4. **Spoke honestly about a bad order.** When he learned of the oath, he said, "My father hath troubled the land." *1 Samuel 14:27–30*
5. **Bound himself to David.** He made a covenant with David and gave him the robe and weapons of a king's son. *1 Samuel 18:1–4*
6. **Stood up for his friend.** He reminded Saul that David had risked his life and done Saul only good. *1 Samuel 19:1–7*
7. **Kept watch and sent a warning.** He tested his father's mood at the new moon feast and warned David with arrows shot in the field. *1 Samuel 20:18–42*
8. **Asked for kindness to his family.** He asked David to show kindness to his house for ever, and they swore, "The LORD be between me and thee, and between my seed and thy seed for ever." *1 Samuel 20:14–17, 42*
9. **Strengthened David in God.** In the wilderness he "strengthened his hand in God" and told him, "thou shalt be king over Israel, and I shall be next unto thee." *1 Samuel 23:16–17*
10. **Stayed by his father.** Though he knew David would be king, he fought beside Saul to the end. *1 Samuel 31:2*

## Across Scripture

*Direct references come first. Thematic echoes are study connections, not places where the text names Jonathan.*

### Old Testament

- **1 Samuel 13–31.** This is the main account of his life.
- **2 Samuel 1:17–27.** David's lament: "Saul and Jonathan were lovely and pleasant in their lives, and in their death they were not divided."
- **2 Samuel 4:4** and **2 Samuel 9:1–13.** His son Mephibosheth is lamed as a child, and David later cares for him "for Jonathan's sake."
- **2 Samuel 21:7, 12–14.** David spares Mephibosheth because of his oath to Jonathan and buries the bones of Saul and Jonathan in Zelah.
- **1 Chronicles 8:33–34; 10:2.** Jonathan's place in Saul's family and his death at Gilboa.
- **Echoes.** "A friend loveth at all times" (Proverbs 17:17). "There is a friend that sticketh closer than a brother" (Proverbs 18:24).

### New Testament

- Jonathan is not named in the New Testament.
- **Echoes.** "Greater love hath no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends" (John 15:13). John the Baptist, who also stepped aside for another, said, "He must increase, but I must decrease" (John 3:30). Paul urges believers to honor one another above themselves (Romans 12:10).

## For discussion

1. **Observe:** What did Jonathan give David when they made their covenant, and what did those gifts mean (1 Samuel 18:3–4)?
2. **Understand:** Jonathan stayed loyal to both Saul and David. How did he manage two loyalties that pulled against each other?
3. **Apply:** Jonathan "strengthened his hand in God" when David was hunted and discouraged. Who needs that kind of visit or message from you this week?
4. **Apply:** Is there someone whose gifts or success could make you feel threatened? What would it look like to act as Jonathan did toward them?
