# Solomon

*The king who asked God for wisdom, built the temple, and let his heart be turned away.*

Solomon was the son of David and Bathsheba, and the third king of Israel. Early in his reign God appeared to him in a dream and said, "Ask what I shall give thee." Solomon asked for an understanding heart to judge God's people, and God gave him wisdom, along with riches and honor. He became famous across the world for his wisdom, his proverbs and his wealth. He built the temple in Jerusalem, the house for the ark and for God's name that David had longed to build. Israel lived in peace and plenty. But Solomon married many foreign wives, and in his old age they turned his heart toward other gods. His life is a warning that a great beginning does not guarantee a faithful ending.

## The life of Solomon

*Dates are approximate (BC). Solomon's age when he became king is not given. His son Rehoboam was 41 when Solomon died (1 Kings 14:21), so Solomon was already a father when he was crowned.*

**In Jerusalem — Born and named.** After the death of David and Bathsheba's first child, Solomon is born. "The LORD loved him," and Nathan the prophet brings the name Jedidiah. *2 Samuel 12:24–25*

**c. 970 BC — Anointed king.** When his older brother Adonijah claims the throne, Nathan and Bathsheba remind David of his promise. Solomon rides David's mule to Gihon and is anointed king. *1 Kings 1:5–53*

**c. 970 BC · Early reign — Secures the throne.** David charges him to walk in God's ways. After David dies, Solomon executes Adonijah and Joab, banishes Abiathar the priest, and later has Shimei put to death for breaking his oath. *1 Kings 2:1–46*

**Early reign — Asks for wisdom.** At Gibeon God appears in a dream and says, "Ask what I shall give thee." Solomon asks for an understanding heart. God promises wisdom, and riches and honor as well. *1 Kings 3:4–15*

**c. 966 BC · 4th year — Begins the temple.** In the 480th year after the Exodus, Solomon begins building the house of the LORD on Mount Moriah, using cedar from Hiram of Tyre. *1 Kings 5:1–6:1; 2 Chronicles 3:1*

**c. 959 BC · 11th year — Temple finished and dedicated.** After seven years the temple is finished. The ark is brought in, the glory cloud fills the house, and Solomon prays before all Israel. *1 Kings 6:37–38; 8:1–66*

**Later years — His heart turns.** Solomon has seven hundred wives and three hundred concubines. "When Solomon was old," they turn his heart after other gods, and God says the kingdom will be torn from his son. *1 Kings 11:1–13*

**c. 931 BC · 40th year — Dies.** Adversaries rise, including Jeroboam, whom the prophet Ahijah promises ten tribes. Solomon dies after forty years and is buried in the city of David. *1 Kings 11:14–43*

## Key activities

*Christians have long linked Solomon with Ecclesiastes, which is written by "the Preacher, the son of David, king in Jerusalem" (Ecclesiastes 1:1) but does not name him. Many scholars think a later writer wrote it in Solomon's voice. Views also differ on how much of Proverbs and the Song of Solomon he wrote himself.*

1. **Loved the LORD.** He walked in the statutes of David, "only he sacrificed and burnt incense in high places." *1 Kings 3:3*
2. **Asked for wisdom, not riches.** God was pleased that he did not ask for long life, wealth or his enemies' lives. *1 Kings 3:5–14*
3. **Judged between two mothers.** He called for a sword to divide a baby, and the true mother gave up her claim to save the child. *1 Kings 3:16–28*
4. **Spoke and wrote wisely.** He spoke three thousand proverbs and a thousand and five songs, and taught about plants and animals. *1 Kings 4:29–34*
5. **Ruled in peace.** Judah and Israel dwelt safely, "every man under his vine and under his fig tree." *1 Kings 4:20–25*
6. **Built the temple.** It was built of stone prepared at the quarry, so no hammer or axe was heard at the site. *1 Kings 6:1–38*
7. **Prayed at the dedication.** "Behold, the heaven and heaven of heavens cannot contain thee; how much less this house that I have builded?" *1 Kings 8:22–53*
8. **Received a warning.** God appeared a second time, promising blessing for obedience and ruin for turning to other gods. *1 Kings 9:1–9*
9. **Built great wealth and a navy.** He built ships at Ezion-geber, brought gold from Ophir, and gathered horses and chariots. *1 Kings 9:26–28; 10:14–29*
10. **Hosted the Queen of Sheba.** He answered all her hard questions. *1 Kings 10:1–13*
11. **Built altars for foreign gods.** For his wives he built high places for Chemosh and Molech on the hill facing Jerusalem. *1 Kings 11:4–8*

## Across Scripture

*Direct references come first. Thematic echoes are study connections, not places where the text names Solomon.*

### Old Testament

- **1 Kings 1–11** and **2 Chronicles 1–9.** These are the two main accounts of his reign.
- **2 Samuel 7:12–16** and **1 Chronicles 22:6–10.** God promises David a son who will build the temple, a "man of rest."
- **Proverbs 1:1; 10:1; 25:1.** Collections of proverbs are credited to him. Hezekiah's men copied some of them.
- **Song of Solomon 1:1** and **Psalms 72; 127.** These carry his name in their titles.
- **Nehemiah 13:26.** Nehemiah warns that "even him did outlandish women cause to sin."
- **Echoes.** Moses warned that Israel's king must not gather many horses, many wives, or great silver and gold (Deuteronomy 17:16–17).

### New Testament

- **Matthew 1:6–7.** He is in the line from David to Jesus.
- **Matthew 6:28–29** and **Luke 12:27.** "Even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these."
- **Matthew 12:42** and **Luke 11:31.** "A greater than Solomon is here."
- **John 10:23; Acts 3:11; 5:12.** Jesus walks, and the apostles later gather, in "Solomon's porch" at the temple.
- **Acts 7:47–48.** Stephen says, "But Solomon built him an house," yet God does not dwell in temples made with hands.

## For discussion

1. **Observe:** What did Solomon ask for at Gibeon, and what did God give him (1 Kings 3:5–14)?
2. **Understand:** Solomon had great wisdom, yet his heart turned away. What is the difference between being wise and staying faithful?
3. **Apply:** Solomon's heart turned slowly, over many years. What small compromise could lead you somewhere you do not want to go?
4. **Apply:** If God said to you, "Ask what I shall give thee," what would you ask for, and why?
