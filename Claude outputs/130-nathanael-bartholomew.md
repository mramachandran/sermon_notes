# Nathanael (Bartholomew)

*The honest skeptic who met Jesus and was promised greater things.*

Nathanael came from Cana in Galilee. When his friend Philip told him the Messiah was Jesus of Nazareth, Nathanael scoffed: "Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth?" But he went to see. Jesus greeted him as "an Israelite indeed, in whom is no guile" and told him he had seen him under the fig tree. Nathanael's doubt turned at once into one of the earliest confessions in John's Gospel: "thou art the Son of God; thou art the King of Israel." John names him only twice. The first three Gospels never mention a Nathanael, but they list an apostle named Bartholomew beside Philip. For that reason many Christians have long believed the two names belong to one man.

## The life of Nathanael (Bartholomew)

*Dates are approximate (AD). Jesus' ministry is dated c. AD 27/28–30 in this study. Entries marked "as Bartholomew" depend on the two names being the same man.*

**c. AD 27–28 · Galilee — Hears about Jesus.** Philip finds Nathanael and says they have found the one Moses and the prophets wrote of, Jesus of Nazareth. Nathanael doubts that anything good can come from Nazareth. *John 1:45–46*

**c. AD 27–28 · Galilee — Meets Jesus.** Jesus says he saw Nathanael under the fig tree before Philip called him. Nathanael confesses him as the Son of God and King of Israel. *John 1:47–51*

**c. AD 28 · As Bartholomew — Chosen as an apostle.** After a night of prayer, Jesus names twelve apostles. Bartholomew is listed next to Philip. *Luke 6:12–16*

**c. AD 30 · Sea of Tiberias — Sees the risen Lord.** Nathanael of Cana is among seven disciples who go fishing with Peter, catch nothing all night, and meet the risen Jesus on the shore at dawn. *John 21:1–14*

**c. AD 30 · As Bartholomew — Waits and prays.** Bartholomew is with the apostles in the upper room after the ascension, waiting for the promised Spirit. *Acts 1:13–14*

### The world around them

Cana was a village in Galilee, a few miles north of Nazareth. Its exact site is debated. Jesus turned water into wine there at a wedding, and from there he healed a nobleman's son who lay sick in Capernaum (John 2:1–11; 4:46–54). Nazareth was a small, obscure village that the Old Testament never mentions. That may explain Nathanael's scorn, though some think it was simple rivalry between neighboring villages. Sitting under one's own fig tree was a familiar picture of peace and safety in Israel.

## Key activities

*Nathanael appears only in John, and Bartholomew appears only in the lists of the Twelve. The link rests on three clues: Bartholomew is a family name ("son of Tolmai") that would allow a personal name as well, he is paired with Philip in three lists, and John counts Nathanael among the disciples. Scripture never says directly that they are one person.*

1. **Questioned honestly.** He asked, "Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth?" *John 1:46*
2. **Came and saw.** Despite his doubt, he went with Philip to meet Jesus. *John 1:46–47*
3. **Was known before he came.** Jesus knew his character and had seen him under the fig tree. *John 1:47–48*
4. **Confessed Jesus.** He called Jesus "the Son of God" and "the King of Israel." *John 1:49*
5. **Received a promise.** Jesus told him he would see heaven open and angels ascending and descending upon the Son of man. *John 1:50–51*
6. **Met the risen Christ.** He was one of the seven by the sea when Jesus appeared. *John 21:1–2*

### Words and places

- **Guile.** Deceit or trickery. Jesus says Nathanael has none (John 1:47).
- **Cana.** Nathanael's village in Galilee, where Jesus did his first sign (John 2:11; 21:2).
- **Bartholomew.** From Aramaic *bar Talmai*, "son of Tolmai" (Matthew 10:3).
- **Son of man.** The title Jesus most often used for himself, drawn from Daniel 7:13 (John 1:51).

## Across Scripture

*Direct references come first. Thematic echoes are study connections, not places where the text names Nathanael.*

### Old Testament

- **Genesis 28:12.** Jacob's ladder; Jesus uses its words to Nathanael (John 1:51)
- **Echo: Genesis 27:35.** Jacob took the blessing "with subtilty"; Nathanael has no guile
- **Echo: 1 Kings 4:25; Micah 4:4.** Sitting under one's fig tree, a picture of peace

### New Testament

- **John 1:45–51.** Nathanael meets Jesus
- **John 21:2.** Among the seven who see the risen Jesus by the sea
- **Matthew 10:3; Mark 3:18; Luke 6:14; Acts 1:13.** Bartholomew listed among the Twelve
- **Tradition.** Eusebius links Bartholomew with India; others say Armenia and martyrdom. Not in Scripture

## For discussion

1. **Observe:** How did Nathanael describe Jesus after their short conversation (John 1:49)?
2. **Understand:** Jesus praised Nathanael's lack of guile even though he had just spoken bluntly about Nazareth. What does this show about honesty?
3. **Apply:** Nathanael brought his doubts to Jesus instead of staying away. What question would you like to bring to Jesus?
4. **Apply:** Jesus knew Nathanael before they met. How does it help you this week to know that God sees you under your "fig tree"?
