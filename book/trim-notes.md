# Trim notes: 23 September 2026

160 chapters were shortened so every page fits the 4-page PDF. Only cuts or condensing: no new facts, dates or references were added, and every KJV quotation was checked against the pre-trim text. Tradition and debated-view labels were kept, often in shorter form.

## Please double-check
- **094 Isaiah:** the Isaiah 9:6 titles had been cut and were restored in full. The note on who wrote chapters 40–66 is now one sentence.
- **063 Goliath:** the view that Elhanan was another name for David was cut. The line now only says 2 Samuel 21:19 is debated.
- **106 Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego:** the key verse now starts at "Our God …" and leaves out "If it be so."
- **094 Isaiah:** the tradition that he was "sawn in two" no longer names Manasseh.
- **156 Paul:** he still says Hebrews is debated, but no longer that most scholars doubt Paul wrote it. The council and the 2nd and 3rd journeys are merged into one timeline entry.
- **137 Mary Magdalene:** the Gregory tradition is now one line. Removed: the 1969 calendar change, the legends and the Gospel of Mary.
- **128 John, 125 Peter, 127 James:** early-church traditions (Irenaeus, upside-down cross, Santiago) are shortened.
- **112 Mordecai** (2 Maccabees and Purim) and **115 Nehemiah** (Ezra–Nehemiah as one book): these tradition notes were removed completely.
- **122 Herod the Great:** the Augustus "pig" saying is now paraphrased, not in quotation marks.
- **072 Absalom:** added the KJV phrase "turned not after Absalom" (1 Kings 2:28). The wording is correct, but the phrase is new.

## Commonly dropped
- Weaker "Echo" cross-references on page 4, in most chapters.
- "X is not named in the Old Testament" filler lines.
- Some key verses shortened with "…" between exact KJV fragments.

## Not shown in the web or PDF output (still in the source files)
Short-chapter extras such as "The world around them", "Words and places", "Read it yourself" and loose *Tradition.* paragraphs. The new build doesn't display them, so decide whether to keep or delete them.
