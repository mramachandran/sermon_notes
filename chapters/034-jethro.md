# Jethro

*The priest of Midian who welcomed a fugitive, rejoiced in God's rescue, and gave Moses wise advice.*

Jethro was a priest in the land of Midian and the father of seven daughters. When a young Egyptian-looking stranger defended his daughters at a well, Jethro invited him home, gave him his daughter Zipporah, and employed him as a shepherd for forty years. That stranger was Moses. Later, after the Exodus, Jethro brought Moses' wife and sons to the camp near Sinai. He listened to the story of Israel's rescue, praised the LORD, and offered sacrifices. The next day he saw Moses judging the people from morning to evening and gave him practical advice that shaped Israel's leadership. He was an outsider, yet he worshipped Israel's God and blessed Israel's leader.

## The life of Jethro

*Dates are approximate (BC) and follow the early Exodus date, c. 1446 BC. On the late date, move them about 185 years later. Exodus 18 may be placed out of order: Deuteronomy 1:9–15 links the choosing of judges to the time Israel was about to leave Horeb.*

**c. 1486 BC · Moses age 40 — Welcomes a stranger.** His daughters come home early and say an Egyptian rescued them from the shepherds. Jethro asks why they left the man behind and invites him to eat bread. *Exodus 2:15–20*

**c. 1486 BC · Soon after — Gives his daughter.** Moses agrees to live with him. Jethro gives him his daughter Zipporah as a wife, and she bears a son, Gershom. *Exodus 2:21–22*

**c. 1486–1446 BC · Forty years — Employs Moses.** Moses keeps Jethro's flock in the desert. It is while tending this flock that Moses sees the burning bush at Horeb. *Exodus 3:1; Acts 7:29–30*

**c. 1446 BC · Moses leaves — Blesses his return.** Moses asks to go back to his people in Egypt. Jethro answers, "Go in peace." *Exodus 4:18*

**c. 1446 BC · Near Sinai — Visits the camp.** Hearing all that God has done, he brings Zipporah and her two sons to Moses. He rejoices, praises the LORD, and offers sacrifices. *Exodus 18:1–12*

**c. 1446 BC · The next day — Advises Moses and goes home.** He sees Moses judging all day and suggests he appoint capable leaders under him. Moses agrees, and Jethro returns to his own land. *Exodus 18:13–27*

### The world around them

The Midianites were descendants of Abraham through Keturah (Genesis 25:1–2). They lived mainly east of the Gulf of Aqaba, in what is now northwest Saudi Arabia, and moved with their flocks. Midianite merchants had once carried Joseph down to Egypt (Genesis 37:28, 36). Later, Midian became Israel's enemy (Numbers 31; Judges 6). Jethro's family, however, stayed friendly to Israel. His descendants, the Kenites, lived among the tribe of Judah and were spared by Saul in his war with the Amalekites (1 Samuel 15:6).

## Key activities

*His names are debated. Reuel may be a family or clan name. Numbers 10:29 speaks of "Hobab, the son of Raguel," and Judges 4:11 calls Hobab Moses' father-in-law. Some think Hobab was Moses' brother-in-law.*

1. **Showed hospitality.** He scolded his daughters for leaving their rescuer at the well and brought Moses home to eat. *Exodus 2:20*
2. **Took in a fugitive.** He gave Moses a home, a wife and work for forty years. *Exodus 2:21; 3:1*
3. **Let Moses go in peace.** When Moses wanted to return to Egypt, Jethro did not hold him back. *Exodus 4:18*
4. **Reunited a family.** He brought Zipporah, Gershom and Eliezer back to Moses. *Exodus 18:2–6*
5. **Praised Israel's God.** He said, "Blessed be the LORD, who hath delivered you." *Exodus 18:9–11*
6. **Worshipped with Israel.** He offered a burnt offering, and Aaron and the elders ate bread with him "before God." *Exodus 18:12*
7. **Told Moses the hard truth.** "The thing that thou doest is not good." He urged Moses to teach the people and share the judging. *Exodus 18:17–23*

*Tradition.* The Druze honor Jethro as a chief prophet, and many Muslim writers link him with the prophet Shu'ayb. Scripture calls him only a priest of Midian.

### Words and places

- **Midian.** A desert region east of the Gulf of Aqaba, named after a son of Abraham (Genesis 25:2; Exodus 2:15).
- **Priest of Midian.** It is not stated which god or gods he served before meeting Israel's God (Exodus 3:1; 18:1, 11).
- **Kenites.** A clan related to Jethro that settled among Israel (Judges 1:16; 1 Samuel 15:6).
- **Mount of God.** Horeb, also called Sinai, where Moses met God and Jethro met Moses (Exodus 3:1; 18:5).

## Across Scripture

*Direct references come first. Thematic echoes are study connections, not places where the text names Jethro.*

### Old Testament

- **Exodus 2:15–22; 3:1; 4:18.** He welcomes Moses and blesses his return to Egypt
- **Exodus 18:1–27.** His visit, praise, sacrifice and advice
- **Numbers 10:29–32.** Moses invites Hobab, "the son of Raguel," to come along
- **Deuteronomy 1:9–18.** Moses recalls appointing leaders to share the load
- **Judges 1:16; 4:11; 1 Samuel 15:6.** His descendants, the Kenites, live alongside Israel

### New Testament

- Jethro is not named in the New Testament.
- **Acts 7:29.** Moses "a stranger in the land of Madian"
- **Echo: Joshua 2:9–11; 1 Kings 10:9.** Other outsiders praise Israel's God
- **Echo: Acts 6:1–4.** The apostles appoint seven to share the work

## For discussion

1. **Observe:** What did Jethro do when he heard what God had done for Israel (Exodus 18:9–12)?
2. **Understand:** Why do you think Moses listened to advice from his father-in-law, a priest of Midian?
3. **Apply:** Jethro told Moses, "Thou wilt surely wear away." Where are you carrying too much alone, and what could you hand to others?
4. **Apply:** Who is a wise older person who could speak honestly into your life? When could you ask for their advice?
