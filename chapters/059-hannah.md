# Hannah

*The childless woman who poured out her soul to God and gave her son Samuel back to him.*

Hannah was one of the two wives of Elkanah of Ephraim. Her rival, Peninnah, had children, but Hannah had none, and every year at Shiloh Peninnah provoked her until she wept. One year Hannah prayed in deep distress and vowed that if God gave her a son, she would give him to the LORD for life. Eli thought she was drunk, then blessed her. God remembered Hannah, and she bore Samuel. When he was weaned she left him at Shiloh and sang a song of praise later echoed in Mary's song.

## The life of Hannah

*Dates are rough; the house chronology places Samuel's birth c. 1100 BC.*

**Year by year — Provoked at Shiloh.** Elkanah loves Hannah, but Peninnah provokes her because the LORD has shut up her womb. Hannah weeps and will not eat. *1 Samuel 1:1–9*

**At the temple — Prays and vows.** In bitterness of soul she vows to give a son to the LORD for life. Eli thinks she is drunk, then blesses her. *1 Samuel 1:10–18*

**c. 1100 BC · At Ramah — Samuel is born.** She bears a son and names him Samuel, "Because I have asked him of the LORD." *1 Samuel 1:19–23*

**After weaning — Gives Samuel to the LORD.** She brings him to Shiloh and tells Eli, "For this child I prayed," lending him to the LORD. *1 Samuel 1:24–28*

**That day — Sings her song.** She rejoices in God's salvation: he humbles the proud, raises the poor, and will exalt his anointed. *1 Samuel 2:1–10*

**Each year after — More children.** She brings Samuel a little coat each year. The LORD visits her, and she bears three sons and two daughters. *1 Samuel 2:18–21*

## Key activities

*Hannah speaks of God exalting "the horn of his anointed" (1 Samuel 2:10) before Israel has any king. Some read this as looking ahead to Israel's kings, others as pointing beyond them to the Messiah; many see both.*

1. **Worshipped faithfully.** She went up to Shiloh year after year, even though the trip brought her pain. *1 Samuel 1:3–7*
2. **Endured provocation.** Peninnah "provoked her sore," yet Scripture records no reply from Hannah. *1 Samuel 1:6–7*
3. **Poured out her soul.** She brought her grief to God in prayer and tears. *1 Samuel 1:10, 15*
4. **Made a vow.** She promised to give her son to the LORD for life. *1 Samuel 1:11*
5. **Answered respectfully.** When Eli accused her, she explained herself gently and did not argue. *1 Samuel 1:14–16*
6. **Left her burden with God.** After Eli's blessing she ate, "and her countenance was no more sad." *1 Samuel 1:18*
7. **Named her son.** She called him Samuel as a reminder that she had asked him of the LORD. *1 Samuel 1:20*
8. **Kept her vow.** She weaned Samuel, then brought him to Shiloh and left him in the LORD's service. *1 Samuel 1:24–28*
9. **Sang God's praise.** "There is none holy as the LORD: for there is none beside thee." *1 Samuel 2:1–2*
10. **Cared from a distance.** Each year she made Samuel a little coat and brought it to him. *1 Samuel 2:19*

## Across Scripture

*Direct references come first. Thematic echoes are study connections, not places where the text names Hannah.*

### Old Testament

- **1 Samuel 1:1–2:21.** The main account
- **Echo: Genesis 21:1–2; 25:21; 30:22–24.** Sarah, Rebekah, Rachel given children
- **Echo: Judges 13:2–3.** Samson's mother, also once barren
- **Echo: Psalm 113:7–9.** Lifting the poor; "the barren woman" made joyful

### New Testament

- Hannah is not named in the New Testament.
- **Echo: Luke 1:46–55.** Mary's song follows the pattern of Hannah's
- **Echo: Luke 1:7, 13.** Elizabeth, long childless, bears a promised son
- **Echo: Luke 2:36.** Anna bears the Greek form of Hannah's name
- **Echo: 1 Peter 5:7.** "Casting all your care upon him"

## For discussion

1. **Observe:** How did Hannah describe what she was doing when Eli thought she was drunk (1 Samuel 1:15–16)?
2. **Understand:** Hannah asked God for a son, then gave him back. What does that show about how she saw her gift?
3. **Apply:** Hannah took her deepest pain to God in honest prayer. What would it look like for you to pour out your soul to God this week?
4. **Apply:** Is there something God has given you, such as a child, a skill or your time, that you need to offer back to him?
