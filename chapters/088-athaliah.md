# Athaliah

*The daughter of Ahab who seized David's throne and tried to wipe out his line.*

Athaliah was a princess of the northern kingdom, a daughter of King Ahab, married into the royal family of Judah. Through her, the worship of Baal that Ahab's house had spread in Israel came south to Jerusalem. She was queen to one king of Judah and mother and counsellor to the next. When her son Ahaziah was killed in Jehu's revolt, she killed the royal heirs and ruled Judah herself for six years. She is the only woman in Scripture to reign over Judah. Yet one baby grandson was hidden from her in the temple. Her story is a dark one, but it shows God keeping his promise to David when that promise hung by a single thread.

## The life of Athaliah

*Dates are approximate (BC). Scripture does not say when she was born or married, or who her mother was.*

**Before c. 848 BC · Marriage — A royal alliance.** King Jehoshaphat of Judah makes an alliance with Ahab by marriage. Athaliah becomes the wife of his son Jehoram, who walks in the ways of Ahab's house. *2 Kings 8:16–18; 2 Chronicles 18:1; 21:6*

**c. 841 BC · Her son's reign — Counsellor to evil.** Her son Ahaziah becomes king of Judah and reigns one year. Chronicles says his mother was his counsellor to do wickedly. *2 Kings 8:25–27; 2 Chronicles 22:2–4*

**c. 841 BC · Jehu's revolt — Her son is killed.** Jehu, anointed to destroy the house of Ahab, kills Ahaziah along with Joram king of Israel. Jehu also kills Ahaziah's relatives. *2 Kings 9:27; 10:12–14*

**c. 841 BC · Seizes power — Destroys the royal heirs.** Athaliah kills the royal family of Judah and takes the throne. But Jehosheba, a king's daughter and Ahaziah's sister, hides the baby Joash. *2 Kings 11:1–2; 2 Chronicles 22:10–11*

**c. 841–835 BC · Six years — Rules over Judah.** She reigns over the land while Joash is hidden in the house of the LORD. Chronicles says her sons broke into the temple and gave its holy things to Baal. *2 Kings 11:3; 2 Chronicles 24:7*

**c. 835 BC · Seventh year — "Treason, Treason."** Jehoiada crowns Joash in the temple. Athaliah hears the shouting, comes in, and tears her clothes. She is led out and put to death by the horse gate. *2 Kings 11:4–16; 2 Chronicles 23:12–15*

### The world around them

After Solomon the kingdom split in two: Israel in the north and Judah in the south. For years the two were rivals. King Jehoshaphat of Judah chose peace with Ahab of Israel, and the marriage of his son to Ahab's daughter sealed it. Royal marriages like this were common in the ancient Near East, and they often brought the bride's gods with her. Ahab's queen Jezebel had promoted Baal in Israel. Many readers assume she was Athaliah's mother, but the text never says so.

## Key activities

*2 Kings 8:26 calls Athaliah "the daughter of Omri," while 2 Kings 8:18 says Jehoram's wife was Ahab's daughter. In Hebrew, "daughter" can mean a female descendant, so she was probably Ahab's daughter and Omri's granddaughter.*

1. **Married into David's line.** Her marriage tied Judah's royal house to Ahab's, and her husband Jehoram "walked in the way of the kings of Israel." *2 Kings 8:18; 2 Chronicles 21:6*
2. **Counselled her son.** Chronicles says she was Ahaziah's counsellor "to do wickedly." *2 Chronicles 22:3*
3. **Destroyed the royal family.** When her son died, she killed the heirs to the throne so that she could rule. *2 Kings 11:1; 2 Chronicles 22:10*
4. **Reigned six years.** She ruled Judah from Jerusalem, the only woman to do so. *2 Kings 11:3*
5. **Promoted Baal.** A house of Baal stood in Jerusalem with its own priest, Mattan. Her sons stripped the temple of the LORD for Baal. *2 Kings 11:18; 2 Chronicles 24:7*
6. **Cried treason and fell.** She saw the boy king by the pillar, rent her clothes, and cried "Treason, Treason." She was slain outside the temple. *2 Kings 11:13–16*

### Words and places

- **Seed royal.** The royal family, especially those who could inherit the throne (2 Kings 11:1).
- **Jehosheba.** Daughter of King Joram and sister of Ahaziah, called Jehoshabeath in Chronicles. She rescued Joash (2 Kings 11:2; 2 Chronicles 22:11).
- **Baal.** A Canaanite storm god whose worship Ahab promoted in Israel (1 Kings 16:31–33).
- **The testimony.** Probably a copy of God's law given to the new king at his crowning (2 Kings 11:12; Deuteronomy 17:18–19).

## Across Scripture

*Direct references come first. Thematic echoes are study connections, not places where the text names Athaliah.*

### Old Testament

- **2 Kings 8:16–27; 2 Chronicles 21:5–22:4.** Her husband's and son's reigns
- **2 Kings 11:1–20; 2 Chronicles 22:10–23:21.** Her reign and death
- **2 Chronicles 24:7.** Called "that wicked woman"; her sons broke into God's house
- **2 Kings 8:19.** God spares Judah for David's sake, a lasting light
- **Echo: 2 Kings 9:30–37.** Jezebel dies in Jehu's revolt that same year
- **Echo: 2 Samuel 7:16.** David's house and kingdom established for ever

### New Testament

- Athaliah is not named in the New Testament.
- **Echo: Matthew 2:13–16.** Herod tries to kill a royal child, who is kept safe
- **Echo: Matthew 1:1–16.** David's royal line traced to Jesus
