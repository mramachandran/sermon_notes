# Josiah

*The boy king who found the Book of the Law and turned Judah back to God.*

Grandson of Manasseh, the most wicked of Judah's kings, Josiah became its most wholehearted reformer. Named in prophecy three centuries before his birth, he took the throne at eight. When a forgotten scroll of God's Law was found in the temple, he wept, gathered the nation, and led the most thorough return to God in Judah's history.

## The life of Josiah

*Dates are approximate (BC). Years count from the start of his reign.*

**c. 930 BC · 300 yrs before — Prophesied by name.** A man of God at Bethel foretells that a son of David named Josiah will burn priests' bones on Jeroboam's altar. *1 Kings 13:1–3*

**c. 640 BC · Age 8 — Crowned king.** After servants assassinate his father Amon, the people of the land make young Josiah king. *2 Kings 21:23–22:1*

**c. 632 BC · Age 16 — Begins to seek God.** While still young, he begins to seek the God of David his father. *2 Chronicles 34:3*

**c. 628 BC · Age 20 — Purges the land.** He tears down high places, Asherah poles and idols in Judah, Jerusalem and the northern tribes. *2 Chronicles 34:3–7*

**c. 627 BC · 13th year — Jeremiah is called.** The word of the LORD comes to young Jeremiah; Zephaniah also prophesies. *Jeremiah 1:1–2; Zephaniah 1:1*

**c. 622 BC · Age 26 — The Book is found.** Hilkiah finds the Book of the Law in the temple. Josiah hears it, tears his clothes, and consults Huldah. *2 Kings 22:3–20*

**c. 622 BC · 18th year — Covenant and Passover.** He reads the Book to all the people, cleanses the land, and keeps a Passover unmatched since Samuel. *2 Kings 23:1–25*

**c. 609 BC · Age 39 — Death at Megiddo.** Ignoring Pharaoh Neco's warning, he is shot by archers and dies. Jeremiah laments him. *2 Chronicles 35:20–25*

## Key activities

*Kings tells the reforms after the Book is found; Chronicles puts the first purge six years earlier.*

1. **Sought God while young.** His faith began as a personal search at sixteen. *2 Chronicles 34:3*
2. **Purged Judah and the north.** He broke down altars, Asherah poles and images and ground them to dust. *2 Chronicles 34:3–7*
3. **Repaired the temple.** He paid workers so trusted that no accounting was asked. *2 Kings 22:3–7*
4. **Heard the Book and wept.** He tore his clothes when the rediscovered Law was read to him. *2 Kings 22:8–13*
5. **Inquired of Huldah.** Judgment would come, but not in his days, because his heart was tender. *2 Kings 22:14–20*
6. **Renewed the covenant.** He read the whole Book aloud and pledged the nation to keep it. *2 Kings 23:1–3*
7. **Cleansed the temple.** He burned the vessels made for Baal, Asherah and the stars in the Kidron fields. *2 Kings 23:4–7*
8. **Ended the high places.** He deposed idol priests and tore down shrines, including Solomon's. *2 Kings 23:5, 8–9, 13–14*
9. **Stopped child sacrifice and the occult.** He defiled Topheth and removed the mediums and household idols. *2 Kings 23:10–12, 24*
10. **Fulfilled prophecy at Bethel.** He burned bones on Jeroboam's altar but spared the prophet's tomb. *2 Kings 23:15–20*
11. **Kept the great Passover.** There had been none like it since Samuel. *2 Kings 23:21–23; 2 Chronicles 35:1–19*
12. **Fell at Megiddo.** He would not heed Pharaoh Neco's warning, and he was shot and died. *2 Chronicles 35:20–24*

## Across Scripture

*Direct references first. Echoes are study links, not places that name Josiah.*

### Old Testament

- **1 Kings 13:1–3.** Named in prophecy; fulfilled in 2 Kings 23:15–18
- **2 Kings 21:23–23:30.** The main account of his reign
- **2 Chronicles 33:25–35:27.** The parallel account, with the Passover in detail
- **1 Chronicles 3:14–15.** Son of Amon; his four sons
- **Jeremiah 1:2; Zephaniah 1:1.** Prophets of his reign
- **Jeremiah 22:15–16.** Praised for justice to the poor
- **Zechariah 12:11.** Mourning at Megiddo, often linked to his death

### New Testament

- **Matthew 1:10–11.** In Jesus' line; "Jechonias" is his grandson
- **Luke 3:23–38.** Luke traces the line through Nathan, so Josiah is absent
- **Echo: John 2:13–17.** A son of David cleanses the temple
- **Echo: Luke 22:7–20.** The Passover fulfilled

## For discussion

1. **Observe:** What did Josiah do the moment he heard the Book of the Law (2 Kings 22:11–13)?
2. **Understand:** Huldah said judgment would still come. Why do you think Josiah reformed the nation anyway?
3. **Apply:** Josiah broke with his family's pattern. What habit would you like to break with God's help?
4. **Apply:** Is there a "lost book" in your life, such as Scripture or prayer? What is one step toward it this week?
