# The Magi

*Wise men from the east who followed a star, worshipped the child Jesus, and went home another way.*

Matthew tells of "wise men from the east" who arrived in Jerusalem asking for the newborn King of the Jews. They had seen his star and come to worship him, and their question alarmed Herod. Micah's prophecy pointed them to Bethlehem, where they bowed down and gave the child gold, frankincense and myrrh. Warned in a dream, they went home another way. Matthew does not say how many there were, name them, or call them kings; those details are later tradition. Foreigners were among the first to worship Jesus, while Jerusalem's scholars are not said to have gone.

## The lives of the Magi

*Dates are approximate (BC). Herod's order about boys "two years old and under" suggests the visit came some months after the birth.*

**In the east — They see the star.** The wise men see a star they link to the birth of the King of the Jews. They set out on a long journey to find and worship him. *Matthew 2:1–2*

**c. 6–4 BC · Jerusalem — They ask for the King.** They arrive in Jerusalem asking where the newborn King is. Herod is troubled, and all Jerusalem with him. The chief priests and scribes point to Bethlehem from the prophet Micah. *Matthew 2:1–6; Micah 5:2*

**c. 6–4 BC · Before Herod — A secret meeting.** Herod calls the wise men privately and finds out exactly when the star appeared. He sends them to Bethlehem, asking them to report back so that he may worship too. *Matthew 2:7–8*

**c. 6–4 BC · Bethlehem — They worship the child.** The star goes before them and stops over the place. Rejoicing greatly, they enter the house, fall down and worship Jesus, and open their gifts. *Matthew 2:9–11*

**c. 6–4 BC · The journey home — Another way.** Warned by God in a dream not to return to Herod, they depart into their own country by another way. *Matthew 2:12*

### The world around them

The Greek historian Herodotus describes the magi as a priestly group among the Medes in Persia. Later the word was used more widely for experts in stars, dreams and hidden knowledge. Daniel had been made chief over the wise men of Babylon centuries before (Daniel 2:48), and Jewish communities remained in the east after the exile. Many think this is how such scholars could have known of Israel's hope of a coming King. Frankincense and myrrh were costly spices from Arabia and East Africa.

## Key activities

*Christians and scholars differ on what the star was: a miracle, a comet, a new star, or a meeting of planets, such as a conjunction of Jupiter and Saturn in 7 BC. Matthew does not explain it.*

1. **Watched and responded.** They noticed a sign in the heavens and acted on it, travelling far to find the King. *Matthew 2:1–2*
2. **Asked openly.** They came to the capital and asked plainly for the newborn King of the Jews. *Matthew 2:2*
3. **Were guided by Scripture.** The prophet's words about Bethlehem showed them where to go next. *Matthew 2:4–6, 9*
4. **Rejoiced.** When they saw the star again, "they rejoiced with exceeding great joy." *Matthew 2:10*
5. **Worshipped the child.** They found him with Mary his mother, fell down and worshipped him. *Matthew 2:11*
6. **Gave costly gifts.** They "presented unto him gifts; gold, and frankincense, and myrrh." *Matthew 2:11*
7. **Obeyed God's warning.** They did not return to Herod but went home another way. *Matthew 2:12*

### Words and places

- **Wise men.** KJV for *magoi*, learned men of the East (Matthew 2:1).
- **Frankincense.** A fragrant resin burned as incense in worship (Exodus 30:34; Matthew 2:11).
- **Myrrh.** A fragrant resin used in perfume, anointing oil and burial (Exodus 30:23; John 19:39).
- **Bethlehem.** The small town of Judaea where David was born. KJV calls it "Bethlehem of Judaea" (Matthew 2:1; 1 Samuel 16:1).

## Across Scripture

*Direct references first. Echoes are study links, not places that name the Magi.*

### Old Testament

- **Micah 5:2.** A ruler from Bethlehem, quoted to Herod (Matthew 2:6)
- **Numbers 24:17.** "A Star out of Jacob," which many link with the star
- **Isaiah 60:3, 6; Psalm 72:10–11.** Kings bring gold and incense; shaped the "kings" idea

### New Testament

- **Matthew 2:1–16.** The only account of them
- **Acts 8:9; 13:6–8.** The same word family for Simon and Elymas the sorcerers
- **Tradition.** Names Caspar, Melchior and Balthasar (c. 6th century) and Epiphany; not Scripture

## For discussion

1. **Observe:** What three gifts did the wise men bring (Matthew 2:11)?
2. **Understand:** The wise men travelled far to worship, while the scribes in Jerusalem knew the prophecy but are not said to have gone. What does this contrast suggest?
3. **Apply:** The wise men gave their best to Jesus. What could you offer him this week, in time, money or talent?
4. **Apply:** They went home "another way." How has meeting Jesus changed the direction of your life?
