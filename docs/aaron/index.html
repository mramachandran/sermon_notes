Aaron

Moses' older brother and spokesman, and the first high priest of Israel.

Aaron was three years older than Moses, and God gave him to his brother as a voice when Moses said he could not speak well. Together they stood before Pharaoh, and Aaron's rod swallowed the magicians' rods. At Sinai he became Israel's first high priest, the man who would bring the people's offerings to God and God's blessing to the people. Yet the same Aaron made the golden calf, joined Miriam in criticizing Moses, and shared Moses' failure at Meribah. His life shows both the dignity and the weakness of priesthood. The New Testament uses his office to explain the work of Christ, a greater high priest.

The life of Aaron

Dates are approximate (BC), on the early Exodus date, c. 1446 BC; the late date moves them about 185 years later. Ages from Exodus 7:7 and Numbers 33:39.

  2. c. 1446 BCAge 83

Across Scripture

Direct references come first. Thematic echoes are study connections, not places where the text names Aaron.

Old Testament

  • Exodus 28–29; Leviticus 8–10His garments and ordination
  • Deuteronomy 9:20Moses prayed for him after the calf
  • Deuteronomy 10:6His death at Mosera, probably near Hor
  • Psalm 77:20God led his people "by the hand of Moses and Aaron"
  • Psalm 133:2His anointing a picture of unity
  • Micah 6:4God sent "Moses, Aaron, and Miriam"

New Testament

  • Luke 1:5Elisabeth is "of the daughters of Aaron"
  • Hebrews 5:4Called of God, "as was Aaron"
  • Hebrews 7:11–28Christ a priest after Melchisedec, not Aaron
  • Hebrews 9:4His budded rod kept in the ark
  • Echo: Hebrews 4:14–16Christ the "great high priest"

For discussion

  1. Observe: How did Aaron explain the golden calf to Moses (Exodus 32:21–24)?
  2. Understand: Why do you think God still made Aaron high priest after the calf?
  3. Apply: Where are you tempted to give people what they want instead of what is right?
  4. Apply: Aaron and Hur held up Moses' hands. Whose hands could you hold up this week?
