About the book

Lives of the Bible is a small-group study of 170 people in Scripture, from Adam to the early church.

One person, four pages

Every chapter follows the same pattern, so a group always knows what to expect:

  1. Overview. Who they were, key facts at a glance, and a key verse.
  2. Timeline. The main moments of their life, each with its reference.
  3. Key activities. What they actually did, numbered and referenced.
  4. Across Scripture. Every Old and New Testament passage that mentions them, plus four discussion questions.

Direct references are kept separate from thematic echoes, and later tradition is labelled so it is never passed off as Scripture.

Using it in a group

One chapter fits one meeting of about 45 minutes. Download the printable PDF from any chapter page and hand out copies, or read along on a phone.

Kindle and paperback

The complete book is being prepared for Kindle and as a paperback in two volumes: The Old Testament and The New Testament. Chapters appear here first, free to read, as they are written.
