Part 6 · The Divided Kingdom · Chapter 89

Joash and Jehoiada

A rescued boy king and the faithful priest who raised him, and what happened when the priest was gone.

Joash was the only royal heir to escape Athaliah's massacre. His aunt Jehosheba hid him in the temple for six years. Her husband, Jehoiada the high priest, then risked everything to crown the seven-year-old and restore David's line. While Jehoiada lived, Joash did well and repaired the temple. But after the old priest died, Joash turned to idols and even had Jehoiada's son stoned in the temple court. Their story asks whether a borrowed faith will hold when the mentor is gone.

The life of Joash and Jehoiada

Dates are approximate (BC). Scripture dates Joash's temple repairs to his 23rd year but does not date Jehoiada's death.

  1. c. 841 BCInfant

    Rescued and hidden

    Athaliah destroys the royal family. Jehosheba steals away baby Joash with his nurse and hides him in the temple for six years.

    2 Kings 11:1–3; 2 Chronicles 22:10–12
  2. c. 835 BCAge 7

    Crowned in the temple

    Jehoiada shows the captains the king's son and crowns him. The people shout, "God save the king."

    2 Kings 11:4–12, 21; 2 Chronicles 23:1–11
  3. c. 835 BCFirst year

    Covenant renewed

    Jehoiada makes a covenant between the LORD, king and people. Athaliah is killed and Baal's house torn down.

    2 Kings 11:13–20; 2 Chronicles 23:12–21
  4. c. 813 BC23rd year

    The temple repaired

    Jehoiada sets a chest with a hole in its lid by the altar, and the gifts pay the workers.

    2 Kings 12:4–16; 2 Chronicles 24:4–14
  5. Later in the reignAge 130

    Jehoiada dies

    The old priest dies full of days. He is buried among the kings, because he had done good in Israel.

    2 Chronicles 24:15–16
  6. After Jehoiada's death

    Joash turns away

    Judah's princes bow to the king, and he listens to them. They leave the temple and serve idols. Joash has Zechariah, Jehoiada's son, stoned for rebuking them.

    2 Chronicles 24:17–22
  7. c. 796 BCAge 47

    Joash is killed

    Hazael of Syria threatens Jerusalem, and Joash buys him off with the temple's treasures. Later his own servants kill him in his bed.

    2 Kings 12:17–21; 2 Chronicles 24:23–27

Key activities

Kings and Chronicles tell Joash's story differently. Kings focuses on the temple repairs and says little about his decline. Chronicles adds the idolatry after Jehoiada's death and the stoning of Zechariah.

  1. 1

    Jehoiada protected the heir

    He and his wife kept the boy hidden in the temple for six years.

    2 Kings 11:3; 2 Chronicles 22:11–12
  2. 2

    Jehoiada crowned the king

    He armed the guards with David's own spears and shields and set the crown and the testimony on Joash.

    2 Kings 11:9–12
  3. 3

    Jehoiada renewed the covenant

    He bound king and people to be the LORD's people and led them in breaking down Baal's house.

    2 Kings 11:17–18
  4. 4

    Joash did right while taught

    He did right "all his days wherein Jehoiada the priest instructed him." The high places remained.

    2 Kings 12:2–3
  5. 5

    Joash pressed for repairs

    He challenged Jehoiada and the priests over the slow work.

    2 Kings 12:6–8; 2 Chronicles 24:5–6
  6. 6

    They set up the chest

    The people gave gladly, and honest workmen restored the house of the LORD.

    2 Kings 12:9–15; 2 Chronicles 24:8–14
  7. 7

    Joash turned to idols

    After Jehoiada died, the king listened to the princes, and Judah served groves and idols.

    2 Chronicles 24:17–18
  8. 8

    Joash killed Jehoiada's son

    Zechariah warned, "because ye have forsaken the LORD, he hath also forsaken you." He was stoned in the temple court at the king's command.

    2 Chronicles 24:20–22
  9. 9

    Joash paid off Hazael

    He sent the temple's hallowed things and gold to the king of Syria.

    2 Kings 12:17–18

Across Scripture

Direct references come first. Thematic echoes are study connections, not places where the text names Joash or Jehoiada.

Old Testament

  • 2 Kings 11:1–12:21Kings: the crowning and the temple repairs
  • 2 Chronicles 22:10–24:27Chronicles adds Joash's turning after Jehoiada died
  • 2 Chronicles 24:22Joash "remembered not the kindness" and slew his son
  • Jeremiah 29:26A priest "in the stead of Jehoiada"; possibly another man
  • Echo: 1 Samuel 2:18–21Samuel also grew up in God's house
  • Echo: 2 Kings 22:1–2Josiah, another boy king who began well

New Testament

  • Matthew 23:35; Luke 11:51"Zacharias" slain; many say Jehoiada's son, some the prophet
  • Matthew 1:8Joash is skipped in Matthew's genealogy
  • Echo: Hebrews 13:7Remember faithful leaders, "whose faith follow"

For discussion

  1. Observe: What words describe how long Joash did right (2 Kings 12:2)?
  2. Understand: Why do you think Joash's faith did not survive Jehoiada's death?
  3. Apply: Who has been a Jehoiada for you? How could you thank them or pass on what they gave you?
  4. Apply: What would help your faith stand on its own, not only when strong believers are around you?
