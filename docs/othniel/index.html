Othniel

The first of the judges, a proven warrior on whom the Spirit of the LORD came.

Othniel first appears in Caleb's day, capturing Kirjath-sepher and winning Caleb's daughter Achsah as his wife. Years later Israel forgot the LORD, and God gave them into the hand of a foreign king for eight years. When they cried out, God raised up Othniel. The Spirit of the LORD came on him, he judged Israel, went to war, and won. The land had rest forty years. His short story sets the pattern of the book of Judges: sin, oppression, a cry for help, and a deliverer sent by God.

The life of Othniel

Judges gives no calendar dates. On the early chronology the judges ruled c. 1375–1050 BC, and Othniel comes first, so his work probably falls near the start of that span. Treat any date as rough.

  1. In Caleb's day

    Takes Kirjath-sepher

    Caleb promises his daughter to the man who captures Kirjath-sepher, later called Debir. Othniel takes the city and marries Achsah.

    Joshua 15:15–17; Judges 1:11–13
  2. Soon after

    Achsah asks for springs

    Achsah urges him to ask Caleb for a field. She then asks her father for springs of water, and Caleb gives the upper and nether springs.

    Joshua 15:18–19; Judges 1:14–15
  3. Eight years

    Israel is oppressed

    Israel forgets the LORD and serves the Baals and the groves. God sells them into the hand of Chushan-rishathaim, and they serve him eight years.

    Judges 3:7–8
  4. When Israel cries out

    Raised up to deliver

    The Spirit of the LORD comes on Othniel. He judges Israel, goes out to war, and defeats Chushan-rishathaim.

    Judges 3:9–10
  5. Forty years

    Rest and death

    The land has rest for forty years, and then Othniel dies.

    Judges 3:11

Key activities

"Othniel the son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother" can be read two ways. Some take Othniel as Caleb's younger brother. Others take Kenaz as the younger brother, which would make Othniel Caleb's nephew. Judges 1:1 also places the capture of Debir after Joshua's death, while Joshua 15 tells it within Caleb's story, and readers explain the order differently.

  1. 1

    Took a fortified city

    He captured Kirjath-sepher, one of the towns in Caleb's allotment.

    Joshua 15:15–17
  2. 2

    Married Achsah

    He received Caleb's daughter as the promised reward.

    Judges 1:12–13
  3. 3

    Received land and water

    With Achsah's help the family gained a field and springs.

    Judges 1:14–15
  4. 4

    Was filled by the Spirit

    "The Spirit of the LORD came upon him." He is the first judge of whom this is said.

    Judges 3:10
  5. 5

    Judged Israel

    He led the people before he led them in battle.

    Judges 3:10
  6. 6

    Defeated a foreign king

    "His hand prevailed against Chushan-rishathaim."

    Judges 3:10
  7. 7

    Brought rest

    The land had rest forty years while he lived.

    Judges 3:11

For discussion

  1. Observe: What four things does Judges 3:10 say happened when the Spirit of the LORD came on Othniel?
  2. Understand: Othniel had already proved himself in battle years before. Why do you think God chose someone with that history?
  3. Apply: Judges 2:10 describes a generation that "knew not the LORD." What can you do to pass on faith to the next generation?
  4. Apply: Israel cried out after eight years. Is there an area of your life where you need to stop struggling alone and cry out to God?
