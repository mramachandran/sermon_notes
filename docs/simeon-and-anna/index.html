Part 8 · The Coming of Jesus · Chapter 120

Simeon and Anna

Two faithful people in the temple who had waited long, and knew the Saviour when they saw him.

When Mary and Joseph brought the forty-day-old Jesus to the temple, two people were waiting there. Simeon was a righteous and devout man in Jerusalem. The Holy Ghost had shown him he would not die before he saw the Lord's Christ. Led by the Spirit into the temple that day, he took the baby in his arms and blessed God, for he had seen God's salvation. Anna was an elderly prophetess, a widow for most of her long life, who never left the temple but worshipped with fasting and prayer night and day. She came in at that moment, gave thanks, and told everyone who was waiting for redemption. Together they show what patient, hopeful waiting on God looks like.

The life of Simeon and Anna

Dates are approximate (BC). Luke gives no age for Simeon, and Anna's age can be read two ways.

  1. Anna's youth

    Married, then widowed

    Anna lives with her husband seven years after her marriage. Then he dies, and she remains a widow for the rest of her long life.

    Luke 2:36–37
  2. The long years

    Waiting in the temple

    Anna does not depart from the temple, serving God with fastings and prayers night and day. In Jerusalem, Simeon waits for the consolation of Israel, and the Holy Ghost is upon him.

    Luke 2:25, 37
  3. Earlier

    Simeon's promise

    The Holy Ghost reveals to Simeon that he will not see death before he has seen the Lord's Christ, God's promised anointed King.

    Luke 2:26
  4. c. 6–4 BCForty days

    Simeon holds the child

    Led by the Spirit into the temple as Jesus is presented, Simeon takes him in his arms and blesses God. He then blesses the parents and warns Mary of sorrow to come.

    Luke 2:27–35
  5. c. 6–4 BCThat same hour

    Anna gives thanks

    Anna comes in at that very moment. She gives thanks to the Lord and speaks of the child to all who look for redemption in Jerusalem.

    Luke 2:38

Key activities

Luke 2:37 says Anna was "a widow of about fourscore and four years." This may mean she was 84, or that she had been a widow for 84 years, which would make her over 100.

  1. 1

    Waited with hope

    Simeon looked for the consolation of Israel, trusting the promise God had given him.

    Luke 2:25
  2. 2

    Followed the Spirit's leading

    Simeon came "by the Spirit" into the temple at the very time Jesus was brought in.

    Luke 2:27
  3. 3

    Recognised the Saviour

    He took the baby in his arms and called him God's salvation, "A light to lighten the Gentiles, and the glory of thy people Israel."

    Luke 2:28–32
  4. 4

    Spoke a hard truth

    Simeon told Mary the child was set for the fall and rising again of many in Israel, and "a sword shall pierce through thy own soul also."

    Luke 2:34–35
  5. 5

    Worshipped without ceasing

    Anna served God with fastings and prayers night and day.

    Luke 2:37
  6. 6

    Told others

    Anna gave thanks and spoke of Jesus to all who were looking for redemption.

    Luke 2:38

Across Scripture

Direct references come first. Thematic echoes are study connections, not places where the text names Simeon or Anna.

Old Testament

  • Isaiah 40:1"Comfort ye, comfort ye my people," the hope Simeon awaited
  • Isaiah 49:6"A light to the Gentiles," echoed by Simeon
  • Echo: 1 Samuel 1:9–18Hannah, whose name Anna shares, prays at God's house
  • Echo: Genesis 46:30Jacob content to die once he has seen Joseph

New Testament

  • Luke 2:22–38The only account of them
  • Luke 24:21Disciples hoped Jesus would "have redeemed Israel"
  • 1 Timothy 5:5A widow praying "night and day," as Anna did
  • TraditionSimeon as a very old man or a priest; Luke says neither

For discussion

  1. Observe: What had the Holy Ghost promised Simeon (Luke 2:26)?
  2. Understand: Simeon calls Jesus a light to the Gentiles and the glory of Israel. Why is it important that Jesus came for both?
  3. Apply: Anna waited and prayed for decades, and Simeon held on to God's promise. What helps you keep hoping in God while you wait?
  4. Apply: Anna told others about Jesus as soon as she met him. Who is one person you could tell about what God has done for you?
