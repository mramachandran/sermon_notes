Zedekiah

Judah's last king, who kept asking for God's word but never found the courage to obey it.

Zedekiah was a son of good King Josiah. Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon put him on the throne after carrying his nephew Jehoiachin into exile, and even gave him his royal name. For eleven years he ruled a shrinking kingdom caught between Babylon and Egypt. Again and again he sent for Jeremiah and asked, in secret, whether there was any word from the LORD. There was, and it did not change: submit to Babylon and live. But Zedekiah feared his own officials more than he trusted God. He broke his oath to Nebuchadnezzar and rebelled. Jerusalem fell, the temple burned, and Zedekiah was blinded and led away to Babylon in chains.

The life of Zedekiah

Dates are approximate (BC) and agree with the Josiah and Jeremiah chapters. Some date the fall of Jerusalem to 587 BC. Scripture does not say when Zedekiah died.

  1. c. 597 BCAge 21

    Made king by Babylon

    Nebuchadnezzar takes Jehoiachin to Babylon and makes his uncle Mattaniah king, changing his name to Zedekiah. He makes him swear by God to stay loyal.

    2 Kings 24:15–18; 2 Chronicles 36:10–13
  2. c. 594 BC4th year

    Tempted to rebel

    Envoys from nearby kingdoms come to Jerusalem. Jeremiah wears a yoke and tells Zedekiah to serve the king of Babylon and live.

    Jeremiah 27:3, 12–15; 28:1
  3. c. 589 BCLate in his reign

    Rebels

    Zedekiah breaks his oath and rebels against Nebuchadnezzar, apparently hoping for help from Egypt.

    2 Kings 24:20; 2 Chronicles 36:13; Ezekiel 17:15
  4. c. 588 BC9th year

    The siege begins

    Nebuchadnezzar's army surrounds Jerusalem and builds siege works around it.

    2 Kings 25:1; Jeremiah 39:1
  5. c. 588–586 BCDuring the siege

    Secret meetings

    Zedekiah asks Jeremiah privately for God's word and is told to surrender. He lets his officials throw Jeremiah into a cistern, then allows his rescue.

    Jeremiah 37:16–21; 38:1–28
  6. c. 586 BC11th year

    Flight and capture

    Famine grips the city and the wall is broken. Zedekiah flees by night but is caught in the plains of Jericho. At Riblah his sons are killed before his eyes, and he is blinded.

    2 Kings 25:2–7; Jeremiah 39:2–7
  7. After c. 586 BCIn Babylon

    Dies a prisoner

    He is taken to Babylon in chains and kept in prison until the day of his death.

    Jeremiah 52:11

Key activities

2 Kings 24:17 calls Zedekiah Jehoiachin's "father's brother," while 2 Chronicles 36:10 calls him his "brother," a word that can mean any close relative. Many scholars think Zedekiah's royal name echoes Jeremiah's promise of a coming king called "THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS" (Jeremiah 23:5–6).

  1. 1

    Swore loyalty to Babylon

    He took an oath in God's name to serve Nebuchadnezzar.

    2 Chronicles 36:13; Ezekiel 17:13
  2. 2

    Sent to inquire of the LORD

    He asked Jeremiah to pray for the city and to ask whether God would save it.

    Jeremiah 21:1–2; 37:3
  3. 3

    Freed the slaves, then took them back

    He led Jerusalem in a covenant to free Hebrew slaves, but the owners soon took them back.

    Jeremiah 34:8–11
  4. 4

    Asked in secret

    He brought Jeremiah from prison and asked, "Is there any word from the LORD?"

    Jeremiah 37:17
  5. 5

    Gave way to his officials

    He handed Jeremiah over to the princes, who put him in a cistern.

    Jeremiah 38:4–6
  6. 6

    Feared people more than God

    He admitted he was afraid of the Jews who had gone over to the Babylonians.

    Jeremiah 38:19–20
  7. 7

    Rebelled and fled

    He broke his oath, and when the city fell he ran and was captured.

    2 Kings 24:20–25:6

For discussion

  1. Observe: What question did Zedekiah ask Jeremiah, and what was the answer (Jeremiah 37:17)?
  2. Understand: Zedekiah kept asking for God's word. Why do you think he never acted on it?
  3. Apply: Is there something God has already made clear to you that you are still hoping he will change? What would obedience look like?
  4. Apply: Whose opinion do you fear most? How could you keep that fear from steering your choices this week?
